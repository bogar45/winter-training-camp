# Shot Tracker

A fast, offline iPhone home-screen app for logging makes and misses by spot on the floor.

## Install on iPhone
1. Open `https://bogar45.github.io/winter-training-camp/shots/` in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open it from the home-screen icon. After the first load it works with no signal.

## Use
- **Add player**, then tap the player and choose **Start session**.
- Tap the spot on the court, then tap **MAKE** or **MISS** for each shot. Tap a new spot when the player moves.
- **Undo last** removes the most recent shot. **End** saves the session.
- Several players can have live sessions at once. Switch between them with the name chips at the top.
- The player page shows FG/FT/2PT/3PT %, a court heat map, % by session over time (tap a spot to chart just that spot), per-spot trend lines, and session history.

## Data
Everything is stored on the phone. Use **⚙︎ → Export backup** now and then to keep a copy.
